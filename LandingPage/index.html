Public Code of Conduct

Kotlin User Group Chennai, India

Our community is built on trust, respect, and accountability. This code applies to all events, online spaces, and team interactions.

Kotlin User Group Chennai is a volunteer-run community focused on learning, sharing knowledge, and building great things with Kotlin and modern technologies in Chennai, India. Our events and community spaces are welcoming, safe, and inclusive for everyone regardless of experience level, background, or identity.

Scope of Applicability

This Code of Conduct applies to all KUG Chennai spaces and interactions:

  • Meetups and tech talks
  • Hackathons (KoHacks)
  • Workshops and Kotlin Jams
  • Online communities such as Discord and WhatsApp
  • Social media platforms like Instagram and official channels
  • Any space where someone represents Kotlin User Group Chennai

Applies to attendees, speakers, volunteers, organizers, sponsors, and community members.

Expected Behaviour

  • Be respectful and considerate to everyone.
  • Encourage learning, collaboration, and healthy discussions.
  • Be welcoming to developers of all skill levels, including beginners.
  • Respect different opinions, experiences, and perspectives.
  • Give constructive feedback and communicate professionally.
  • Follow venue rules and event schedules during physical events.

Guidelines for Online Communities

Applies to Discord, WhatsApp groups, Instagram interactions, and digital platforms:

  • Keep discussions respectful and relevant to the community.
  • Avoid spam, excessive self-promotion, or unrelated advertising.
  • Do not share offensive, explicit, or harmful content.
  • Respect privacy — do not share personal information of others without consent.
  • Be constructive in discussions and avoid unnecessary arguments or trolling.

Anti-Spam Policy

  • Do not post repeated promotional content, advertisements, or referral links.
  • Avoid sending unsolicited direct messages to members for promotions or recruitment.
  • Sharing job opportunities, events, or resources must be relevant to the community.
  • Repeated posting of identical content may be treated as spam and removed without prior notice.

Unacceptable Behaviour

  • Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination of any kind.
  • Offensive comments related to gender, race, religion, disability, nationality, or identity.
  • Inappropriate physical contact or unwelcome advances.
  • Disrupting talks, workshops, hackathons, or discussions.
  • Recording or photographing someone without consent.
  • Sharing explicit, abusive, or harmful content.
  • Repeatedly ignoring moderator or organizer instructions.

Moderation & Enforcement

Moderators reserve the right to maintain a safe environment by taking action:

  • Issue a warning citing the violated rule.
  • Remove messages or inappropriate content.
  • Temporarily mute or restrict the participant in online communities.
  • Remove or permanently ban the participant from events or groups.

Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it via:

Email: kugchennai@gmail.com
  • Direct message to organizers or moderators
  • Reporting to volunteers during physical events

All reports will be handled confidentially and respectfully.

Our Community Values

Learning together Sharing knowledge openly Supporting beginners Growing Kotlin in Chennai
By participating in KUG Chennai, you agree to uphold this Code of Conduct.
